# Raihat Ummi (رائحة أمي) website design brief

- Design read: Hail families and gift buyers who want home-made bukhoor; warm, proud, quiet luxury.
- Concept spine: "from raw material to the ball of mamool". The page is the recipe being made.
- Delivery tier: cinema.
- Locked palette: night teal #061F23, Aja teal #0D4A52 / #08343A, granite red #A8473A, Najdi ochre #C99A3C, sand #E4D2B5, cream #F4ECDD. These are the client's approved brand colours (brand book), overriding the generic bans.
- Locked type: Raihat Kufi (client's own typeface, headings), Almarai (Arabic body), Jost (Latin).
- Animation mode: animated-website
- Journey shape: single-shot film (Kling 3.0, 15 s, start frame = the real raw materials, end frame = the finished mamool ball), cut on exact frames into four chapters so each chapter owns its scroll length. Seams are consecutive frames of one take.
- Journey: 1 الخامات (five materials on stone) · 2 الخلطة (powder and oil swirl into a ring) · 3 التكوير (the ring folds into a sphere) · 4 المعمول (finished ball, smoke, CTA to the collection).
- World grammar: dark teal stone slab, single warm low light from the left, black seamless background, no text.
- Mobile framing: subject centre-safe; 720p mobile encodes.
- Delivery budget: desktop 18 MB, mobile 9.7 MB.
- Section plan: journey (scroll film) · collection (alternating photo/list split, label artwork per product) · story (wide Aja band + door logo + facts) · order (three-step chevrons + two CTAs) · footer.
- Asset plan: hero film, 4 family photos (Higgsfield), 12 vector labels, approved logos, Najdi triangle ribbon, cover/OG composed from the final frame + approved logo.
- CTA inventory: header "اطلب الحين" (arrow-cut outline), chapter-4 "شوف المجموعة" (rotating wax seal), product "اطلب" (red leaf pill), order "راسلنا على انستقرام" (offset-shadow block), "تابعنا على تيك توك" (dashed frame).
